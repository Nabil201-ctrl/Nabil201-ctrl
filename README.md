## 👨‍💻 About Me  

I am **Nabil Abubakar**, a passionate **Full-Stack Developer** and aspiring **Cybersecurity Engineer**.  
My journey into technology started from pure curiosity—I was the kid who disassembled toys, blenders, and computers just to understand *what was happening beyond the screen*.  

That curiosity transformed into a structured pursuit:  
- 🖥️ I began by scribbling code on paper from random sites, not knowing the languages, but feeling I had written something valuable.  
- 🎮 I explored game engines like Unity and wanted to build my own games.  
- 📚 Over time, I matured into **software engineering**, diving deep into programming languages, building calculators, banking apps, and full systems.  
- 🚀 Now, I have developed **real-world projects** in **web, mobile, backend, and blockchain development**.  

Currently, I am pursuing my degree in **Computer Science**, focusing on **Cybersecurity** while strengthening my profile as a **software engineer**.  
I thrive at the intersection of **logic, design, and security**, where I can build products that are not only functional but also secure and impactful.

- 🌍 Based in Nigeria, working on global-ready projects.
