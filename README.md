## Who Am I?

I am a student of Systems Analysis and Development at the Federal Institute of Science and Technology of Ceará (IFCE). My journey into the world of technology began early: at 14, I started programming small 3D games in Unity, and soon after, I delved into the universe of 2D games with GameMaker. At 15, I discovered my creative streak and worked as a freelance graphic designer, which also fueled my interest in interfaces and visual aesthetics.

At 18, upon entering college, I fully immersed myself in the world of programming. Since then, I have been dedicating my time to studying development areas in depth, with a special focus on Full-stack web development. Despite studying the complete ecosystem, my real strength, and what I most enjoy exploring, is the Front-End, where I can unite logic, creativity, and design.
